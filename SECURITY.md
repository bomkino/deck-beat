# Security

Deck Beat sets your slides to your music on your Mac, and reads and writes only the files you choose: the slides and songs you add, its projects and the videos you export. Please report a security problem privately through this repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**) rather than in a public issue, with the version, what you did, what happened, and the smallest case that shows it. A made-up deck and the demo groove are better than your own material.

Releases are ad-hoc signed and not notarized. A binary is supported only when it is published here with a version, a source revision and checksums.
